## Why I Started This Blog

I've been working with infrastructure and automation for over a decade. In that time, I've lost count of how many blog posts, Stack Overflow answers, GitHub issues, and community-maintained docs got me unstuck at 2 AM during an incident, or saved me days of trial and error on a migration I'd never done before.

That is the open source spirit at its core: collaboration, transparency, and the idea that knowledge grows when you share it. After so many years on the receiving end, I decided it was time to contribute back. Not because I have all the answers, but because some of the problems I've solved, the mistakes I've made, and the patterns I've landed on might save someone else the same headaches.

This blog covers OpenShift (ARO, ROSA), Ansible Automation Platform, Kubernetes (EKS, AKS, IKS), and Advanced Cluster Management (ACM). Real scenarios, with real code.
