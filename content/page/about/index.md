## Diego Mateus

Senior Cloud Consultant.

I work with hybrid cloud infrastructure, mostly around OpenShift, Ansible Automation Platform, OpenShift Virtualization, Satellite, and RHEL.

My interest is in how these fit together at scale: OpenShift and Ansible Automation Platform as the engines for modernizing legacy environments and running cloud-native workloads, plus OpenShift Virtualization, migration strategies, and lifecycle management with Satellite.

This blog is where I share hands-on guides, lessons learned, and the patterns that survived contact with a real environment.

### How I write here

- **Everything is tested before it is published.** Posts come out of a lab I build, break on purpose, and rebuild, not out of summarising a product page.
- **Claims link to public sources.** Anything I say about how a product behaves links to vendor documentation, so you can check it rather than take my word for it.
- **When the product and the documentation disagree, I say so**, with the version I ran and the date I measured it. Software moves, and a finding with no date attached stops being useful fast.
- **I write down what did not work too.** The failed attempt is usually the part that saves somebody else an afternoon.
- **Analysis and opinions here are my own.**

### Topics I write about

- **OpenShift** - ARO, ROSA, OCP, OpenShift Virtualization, cluster lifecycle
- **Ansible Automation Platform** - CasC, EE builds, AWX, DR orchestration
- **Kubernetes** - EKS, AKS, IKS, and other managed distributions (xKS)
- **Multi-Cluster** - Advanced Cluster Management (ACM), fleet management
