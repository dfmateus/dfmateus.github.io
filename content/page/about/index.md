## Diego Mateus

Senior Cloud Consultant at Red Hat.

I lead the delivery of end-to-end cloud infrastructure transformations, leveraging the full Red Hat portfolio (OpenShift, Ansible Automation Platform, OpenShift Virtualization, Satellite, and RHEL) for enterprise customers.

My work focuses on architecting and implementing complex Hybrid Cloud solutions, integrating OpenShift and Ansible Automation Platform as core engines for modernizing legacy environments and scaling cloud-native workloads. I also specialize in OpenShift Virtualization, migration strategies, and lifecycle management using Satellite.

This blog is where I share hands-on guides, lessons learned, and patterns from real-world cloud consulting work.

### Topics I write about

- **OpenShift** - ARO, ROSA, OCP, OpenShift Virtualization, cluster lifecycle
- **Ansible Automation Platform** - CasC, EE builds, AWX, DR orchestration
- **Kubernetes** - EKS, AKS, IKS, and other managed distributions (xKS)
- **Multi-Cluster** - Advanced Cluster Management (ACM), fleet management
